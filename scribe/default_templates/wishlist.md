Guidance only — no `required:` sections, so nothing here is enforced.

A wishlist entry is a want, not a task. It earns its keep by explaining the pull:

- **What you wish existed**, in one sentence.
- **What is annoying today** without it — the concrete friction, not a vague "would be nice".
- **Roughly how big** it feels (an afternoon, a week) and what it would touch.
- If it is actually scheduled work, write a todo instead.
